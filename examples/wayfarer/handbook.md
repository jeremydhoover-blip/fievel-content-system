# Wayfarer fictional handbook

Training material authored for Fievel, reviewed 2026-10-21. Not a real service.

Use “booking” for a reservation. Keep explanations calm and clear. Name the next
action and its destination. The assistant in this exercise cannot access account
records, issue refunds, change dates, or cancel bookings.

Cancellation conditions depend on the policy attached to the booking. Ask the
traveler to open their booking confirmation and find its cancellation terms.
Do not assume a universal cancellation window or fee. Conflicting general articles
do not establish which conditions apply to an individual booking.

Payment declines may have different causes. A declined status alone establishes
no cause. Suggest contacting the bank for details; never ask for a full card number.
There is no guaranteed refund processing time in this handbook. If the assistant
cannot see the refund record, say so and direct the traveler to booking support.
