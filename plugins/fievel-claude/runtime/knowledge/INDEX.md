introductory modules, not comprehensive coverage of every topic in the brief.

For covered conceptual questions, use the relevant bundled module and example
first; they do not require a web request. Company policy still needs authorized
organizational evidence. Current tool behavior or an uncovered topic may require
external verification. Never substitute general educational text for approved
company standards. Expanded modules are draft authored lessons with exercise
criteria; independent expert review and learner testing are still pending.
