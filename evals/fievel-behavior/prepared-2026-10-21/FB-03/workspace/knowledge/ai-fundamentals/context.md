# Context and grounding

Reviewed: 2026-10-21. Original Fievel teaching text; references are not organizational policy.

A model generates a response from available context. Grounding means tying claims to evidence that is actually available. Context windows are finite; retrieval selects relevant material.

Example: A declined payment status does not reveal the bank’s reason.

Common mistakes: Assuming the model can see account records or that retrieved text must be true.

Try it: List the facts known in payment-unknown and the facts still unknown.

Related concepts: Tokens; retrieval; hallucination; MCP.

Reference: https://modelcontextprotocol.io/introduction
