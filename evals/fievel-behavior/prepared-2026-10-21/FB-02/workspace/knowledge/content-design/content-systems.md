# Content systems

Reviewed: 2026-10-21. Original Fievel teaching text; references are not organizational policy.

A content system connects rules, examples, sources, behavior and checks. A style guide is one component.

Example: A booking rule links to the handbook and to a question that checks it.

Common mistakes: Treating a long prompt as a maintained system; calling generated drafts approved.

Try it: Pick one rule. Find its source and write one question that checks it.

Related concepts: Structured content; governance.

Reference: https://design-system.service.gov.uk/
