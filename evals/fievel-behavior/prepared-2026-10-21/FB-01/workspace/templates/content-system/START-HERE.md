# Your content system

These are draft files you own. Empty lists and “To be decided” mean work remains;
passing structural validation does not mean your system is complete or approved.

Tell Fievel: “Resume this folder. Read progress.json and help with the next stage.”

portable package; the folder can be moved to another compatible coding agent.

organizational source keep source_ids empty and status draft. Approval belongs to
the content owner. Review files for private material before sharing or committing.
