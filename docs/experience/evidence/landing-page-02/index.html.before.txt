    <section class="process section wrap" id="how-it-works" aria-labelledby="process-title">
      <div class="section-top"><div><p class="overline">A conversation that becomes a system</p><h2 id="process-title">One useful decision<br>at a time.</h2></div><p>Start with a single product task or bring your existing style guide. Your agent leads the conversation; Fievel supplies the structure, skills and reference material.</p></div>
      <div class="process-grid">
        <ol class="steps">
          <li><span class="step-number">01</span><div><h3>Start with what you know</h3><p>Describe your product, audience and constraints. New to AI content systems? Begin with a fictional example.</p></div></li>
          <li><span class="step-number">02</span><div><h3>Connect the pieces</h3><p>Shape writing standards, source records and behavior rules. Keep unknowns explicit and drafts separate from approved guidance.</p></div></li>
          <li><span class="step-number">03</span><div><h3>Check, review, improve</h3><p>Define realistic scenarios, try saved responses and plan ownership. Open the local dashboard to see the system you have created.</p></div></li>
        </ol>
        <figure class="demo">
          <figcaption><span class="dot"></span> Illustrative conversation · fictional example</figcaption>

    <section class="evaluation-band" id="evaluations" aria-labelledby="evaluations-title"><div class="wrap evaluation-layout">
      <div><p class="overline">Evaluations are part of the system</p><h2 id="evaluations-title">Define good.<br>Then put it to the test.</h2><p>Connect your rules to realistic situations and quality checks. Run literal checks on saved responses, then review meaning and judgment with a person.</p><p class="eval-note">A defined check is not a result. A literal pass is not proof of overall quality. Human questions stay open until reviewed.</p></div>
      <div class="eval-chain" aria-label="Illustrative evaluation connection, not an executed result">
        <div><span class="chain-icon" aria-hidden="true">01</span><section><span class="chain-label">Rule</span><h3>Ask when booking details are missing.</h3></section></div><div><span class="chain-icon" aria-hidden="true">02</span><section><span class="chain-label">Scenario</span><h3>“Can you change my booking?”</h3></section></div><div><span class="chain-icon" aria-hidden="true">03</span><section><span class="chain-label">Human review question</span><h3>Did it request the details it needs?</h3><span class="pill">Defined · not assessed</span></section></div>
