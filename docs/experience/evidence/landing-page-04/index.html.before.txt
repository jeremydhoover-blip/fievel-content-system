<section class="evaluation-band" id="evaluations"><div class="wrap evaluation-layout"><div><h2>Make quality part of the system</h2><p>Decide what a good response should do. Connect each rule to a realistic situation and a check. Try saved responses and review the questions that need human judgment.</p><p class="small">A defined check is not a result. Literal checks and human review answer different questions.</p></div><div class="evaluation-example"><div class="evaluation-item"><div><h3>Your standards and behavior</h3><p>Define what the experience should say and do.</p></div></div><div class="evaluation-item"><div><h3>Scenarios and checks</h3><p>Turn those expectations into situations and questions you can assess.</p></div></div><div class="evaluation-item"><div><h3>Evidence and review</h3><p>Inspect saved responses, run literal checks and review meaning with a person.</p></div></div><details class="result-example"><summary>How evaluations work</summary><p>Checks connect to the rules they assess. The local tools run literal checks against saved responses and prepare human review questions. Defined checks, executed results and completed human judgments remain distinct.</p></details></div></div></section>
